STRYKE 2.8 V22 - KAIRO REFERENCE LAYOUT

Base: STRYKE 2.6 V20. The rejected V21 layout was discarded.
Only Kairo is rebuilt in this pass; Vanta, Frostline, Red Dune and Verdant remain on the stable V20 base.

REFERENCE STRUCTURE
- Dense urban block, not an arena with scattered cubes.
- B compound in the northwest, tighter and harder to retake.
- A plaza in the south-center, more open.
- Attacker spawn pocket in the southwest with two meaningful exits.
- Defender spawn on the east side with north and south route choices.
- West flank, north rotation street, central monolith, contested Mid, east S-route and southeast retake lane.
- Real architecture blocks sightlines; no invisible barrier is used as the primary solution.
